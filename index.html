A collection, not a clone

Fourteen games with distinct visual instruments and shared controls. Continue started games, chart your Daily Sky, revisit your Archive, or explore Guided Expeditions.

One signal, worldwide

Official Daily editions are included with the app, selected locally by UTC date and playable offline from the first launch. App updates can add or correct the included content. When no official edition is included for the current UTC date, locally generated Practice remains available without official Daily or streak credit.

Keep playing

Validated generation produces more puzzles when the daily set is complete, without ads, subscriptions, or waiting.

Learn by playing

Concise goals, playable lessons, progressive hints, automatic saving, and spoiler-safe sharing support every game.

Chart your Daily Sky

Start with Orbit, the shared Daily Signal, then explore the full daily set and follow your progress.

Return and explore

Continue games you have started, revisit completed games in your Archive, and explore Guided Expeditions.

Choose your languages

Set interface and puzzle languages separately, with 19 languages supported. Locally generated Unlimited and Practice puzzles work offline.
