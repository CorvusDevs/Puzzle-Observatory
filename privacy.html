Puzzle Observatory

Privacy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Puzzle Observatory stores your progress locally and contains no advertising or cross-app tracking. Daily downloads and support email involve the external services described below.

Data on your device

The app stores puzzle attempts, board snapshots, solve results, streaks, quality ratings, cached Daily editions and preferences on your device. The You tab can export a summary or delete local player records and cached editions. The export includes results, quality ratings, local Echo rejection metrics and preferences, but not guesses, board snapshots or puzzle text. Some app preferences and review/onboarding metadata remain after deletion. Deleting local data does not erase copies you shared, sent support email, browser storage or provider logs.

Diagnostics

Diagnostics are off by default. When enabled, the app records game type and outcome in local system logs and signposts. Apple MetricKit may deliver performance and diagnostic payloads; the app logs only how many payloads arrived and does not save their contents or upload them. This version does not upload guesses, entered words or board contents. No third-party analytics SDK is included.

Daily downloads and Apple services

Official Daily editions are downloaded as signed files from our GitHub Pages site. A new, uncached edition requires an internet connection; authenticated cached editions and locally generated Unlimited puzzles remain playable offline. GitHub receives the network source IP address and requested file, whose path can indicate the puzzle date and language. An IP address routes the internet connection; keeping an IP log is separate from delivering a puzzle. Puzzle Observatory does not create IP logs. Corvus does not access or retain GitHub Pages visitor or IP logs. GitHub states that it stores visitor IP addresses for security, including when visitors are not signed in. We do not set or know the specific retention period of those Pages security logs. The app does not upload guesses, boards, results or player preferences to that host. Game Center and iCloud synchronization are not active in this version. Apple services have their own processing policies.

Feedback and ratings

Choosing Feedback opens your mail app with the app and operating-system versions in an editable message. Puzzle Observatory does not send it automatically or read your sender identity. If you send the message, Corvus support receives its contents, your sender email address, a display name if provided and any details or attachments you choose to include. Your mail app and email providers also process it. Local puzzle-quality ratings stay on your device unless you choose to share an export.

Tracking and collection

The app does not use advertising identifiers or a third-party analytics SDK and does not track you across apps or websites. Local gameplay and diagnostics are not automatically uploaded. Daily requests are still processed by GitHub Pages, which stores IP security logs, and Corvus receives support email only when you choose to send it.

This website

This static website stores your chosen language as puzzle-language in your browser's local storage without a configured expiry. Clearing this site's browser data removes that preference. The site has no analytics script or feedback-upload form. GitHub Pages processes website requests and stores visitor IP addresses for security under its own policy; we do not set its log-retention period.

Support email

Support email and attachments you send are kept only in the corvusdevs@outlook.com support mailbox. Corvus does not forward these messages outside this address. Email-provider backups and logs, your sent copies and GitHub security logs are separate and are not erased by the app's local deletion action. You can contact Corvus support with privacy questions or a request concerning the copies under our control.

Questions

For privacy questions, contact corvusdevs@outlook.com.
