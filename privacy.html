Puzzle Observatory

Privacy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

Puzzle Observatory is designed to work locally and contains no advertising or cross-app tracking.

Data on your device

The app stores puzzle attempts, board snapshots, solve results, streaks, quality ratings, cached daily editions, and preferences so play can resume reliably. This information remains on your device in this version.

Diagnostics

Anonymous quality diagnostics are off by default. If enabled, Apple MetricKit can provide aggregate performance and diagnostic payloads. Structured local events contain only game type and outcome. This version does not upload those payloads.

Daily downloads and Apple services

Official Daily editions are downloaded as signed files from our GitHub Pages site. A new, uncached edition requires an internet connection; downloaded editions and locally generated Unlimited puzzles remain playable offline. Requests reveal your IP address and the requested file, which can identify the puzzle date and language, to the hosting provider. The app does not upload guesses, boards, results, or player preferences. Game Center and private iCloud synchronization are not active in this version.

Feedback and ratings

The app may offer Apple's system rating prompt after sustained use. Choosing Feedback opens your mail app with app and operating system versions prefilled. Email is sent only if you choose to send it.

Tracking and collection

The app privacy manifest declares no tracking and no collected data types. No third-party analytics SDK is included.

Questions

For privacy questions, contact corvusdevs@outlook.com.
